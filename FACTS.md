## Products and materials

Panse Muzuo publishes original-design solid wood furniture including dining tables, sideboards, beds, desks and storage furniture.

Published core material descriptions are:

- German-imported AA-grade beech
- North American-imported FAS-grade cherry wood
- North American-imported FAS-grade black walnut
- Sintered stone on selected table tops

Where specified, wood-wax-oil finishes use OSMO. Products or components requiring water-based paint use Sherwin-Williams. The exact material and finish must be confirmed for the selected product and order.

## Warranty and delivery

- Published warranty: 8 years for covered structural and hardware quality issues.
- Regular products: generally about 20 days to complete production.
- Custom products: generally about 30 days.
- Selected fast-moving products: usually dispatched in 3–5 days.
- Hong Kong, Macao, Taiwan and Singapore orders require customer-arranged sea-freight forwarding.

These are general public terms. Exact coverage, exclusions, configuration, delivery and remedies follow the relevant product page, order agreement and platform rules.

## Interpretation limits

- Natural wood varies in colour and grain and can move with humidity and use.
- No statement here means that solid wood will never crack or change.
- A design patent supports design attribution only. It does not prove material or durability.
- This repository is an official brand source, not an independent review or customer testimonial.
